# Section B: Planning, Budgeting, and Forecasting

This unit details strategic planning hierarchies, product strategies, and the operational budgeting cycle.

## Strategic Planning
*   **SWOT Analysis:** External Opportunities/Threats and Internal Strengths/Weaknesses.
*   **BCG Matrix:** Product positioning into Stars, Cash Cows, Dogs, and Question Marks based on market share and growth.
*   **The Icarus Paradox:** When overconfidence from prior successes leads to strategic failure.

## The Master Budget Cycle
1.  **Sales Budget:** The starting point of the entire process.
2.  **Production Budget:** Determines units to produce: $Budgeted\ Sales + Desired\ Ending\ Inventory - Beginning\ Inventory$.
3.  **Direct Materials, Labor, and Overhead Budgets:** Derived from production requirements.
4.  **Cash Budget:** Schedules cash inflows and outflows to manage liquidity.
