# Section E: Internal Controls

This unit covers risk management, corporate governance, segregation of duties, and SOX compliance.

## COSO Framework (Mnemonic: CRIME)
1.  **C**ontrol Environment (The "tone at the top")
2.  **R**isk Assessment (Identifying and analyzing internal and external risks)
3.  **I**nformation and Communication (Ensuring vital information flows across the firm)
4.  **M**onitoring Activities (Evaluating control performance over time)
5.  **E**xisting Control Activities (Policies and procedures like physical security and authorizations)

## Segregation of Duties (Mnemonic: ARCR)
To prevent fraud and errors, separate these four critical transaction responsibilities:
1.  **A**uthorization (Approving transactions)
2.  **R**ecordkeeping (Maintaining ledger accounts)
3.  **C**ustody (Holding physical assets or cash)
4.  **R**econciliation (Comparing physical counts to ledger balances)

## Sarbanes-Oxley (SOX) Highlights
*   **Section 201:** Restricts audit firms from offering non-audit services to public clients.
*   **Section 302:** CEOs and CFOs must personally certify financial report accuracy.
*   **Section 404:** Management must assess and report on the effectiveness of internal financial reporting controls.
*   **Section 407:** Requires disclosure of whether the Audit Committee includes at least one financial expert.
