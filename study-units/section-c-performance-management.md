# Section C: Performance Management

Performance management relies on standard costing systems and variance analysis to implement "management by exception."

## Establishing Standards
*   **Ideal (Theoretical) Standards:** Assume perfect operating conditions. Can demotivate staff as they are rarely achievable.
*   **Practical (Currently Attainable) Standards:** Allow for normal spoilage, machine downtime, and worker breaks. Highly motivating.
*   **Activity Analysis:** Multidisciplinary study involving engineering, production, and accounting to set realistic benchmarks.

## Variance Formulas
*   **DM Price Variance:** $AQ 	imes (AP - SP)$
*   **DM Usage Variance:** $SP 	imes (AQ - SQ)$
*   **DL Rate Variance:** $AH 	imes (AR - SR)$
*   **DL Efficiency Variance:** $SR 	imes (AH - SH)$
