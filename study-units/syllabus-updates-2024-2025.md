# Syllabus Updates & Advanced Focus (2024-2025)

The updated syllabus emphasizes advanced data analytics, technology infrastructure, and ethics in automated systems.

## Data Analytics & Tech
*   **Regression Analysis:** Modeling relationships using $Y = a + bX$ (where $Y$ is the dependent variable, $a$ is the intercept, $b$ is the slope, and $X$ is the independent variable).
*   **Data Architecture:**
    *   *Data Lake:* Raw, unstructured, and scalable storage.
    *   *Data Warehouse:* Highly structured, cleaned, and optimized for queries.
    *   *Data Mart:* A subset of a warehouse tailored for a specific business unit (e.g., Marketing).

## Ethics in Artificial Intelligence
*   **IMA Ethical Standards:** Transparency, accountability, fairness, and security.
*   **Algorithmic Bias:** Flawed historical training data can lead to discriminatory models.
*   **The Black Box Problem:** Complex deep learning models lack transparency, making auditability difficult.
