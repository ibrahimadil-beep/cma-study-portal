## Variance Formulas
*   **DM Price Variance:** $AQ \times (AP - SP)$
*   **DM Usage Variance:** $SP \times (AQ - SQ)$
*   **DL Rate Variance:** $AH \times (AR - SR)$
*   **DL Efficiency Variance:** $SR \times (AH - SH)$
