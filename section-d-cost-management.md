# Section D: Cost Management
## Traceability & The Object-Dependent Rule
*   **Direct Costs:** Easily and economically traceable to a cost object.
*   **Indirect Costs:** Allocated using causal cost drivers.
*   **The Object-Dependent Rule:** A supervisor's salary is direct to the department, but indirect to a single unit.
