# Case Study 3: Traditional vs. ABC Cost Allocation

## Scenario
A factory produces 10,000 Standard Units and 1,000 Custom Units. Setup costs are $110,000.
*   Standard Units require 2 setups.
*   Custom Units require 9 setups.
*   Traditional volume-based system allocates setup costs based on production units.

## Required Tasks
1.  Calculate setup cost per unit under the Traditional system.
2.  Calculate setup cost per unit under Activity-Based Costing (ABC).
3.  Explain why traditional systems distort product profitability.

## Solution & Rubric
1.  **Traditional Rate:** Setup cost per unit is $\$110,000 / 11,000 	ext{ units} = \$10/	ext{unit}$ for both.
2.  **ABC Rates:** Total setups = 11. Cost per setup = $\$110,000 / 11 = \$10,000/	ext{setup}$.
    *   Standard Units setup cost per unit: $(2 	imes \$10,000) / 10,000 = \$2/	ext{unit}$.
    *   Custom Units setup cost per unit: $(9 	imes \$10,000) / 1,000 = \$90/	ext{unit}$.
3.  **Profitability Distortion:** The traditional system severely undercosts low-volume custom products (\$10 vs. \$90) and overcosts high-volume standard products (\$10 vs. \$2).
