# Case Study 2: COSO Controls & Fraud Scenario

## Scenario
At a mid-sized corporation, the CEO appointed her brother as the head of the Audit Committee. Furthermore, the accounts receivable clerk receives customer checks, records the entries in the subledger, and prepares the monthly bank reconciliation.

## Required Tasks
1.  Identify internal control deficiencies under the COSO Framework.
2.  Analyze how the **ARCR** segregation rule is being violated.
3.  Suggest corrective actions.

## Solution & Rubric
1.  **Control Environment Weakness:** The Audit Committee lacks independence since the head is the CEO's brother (violating SOX Section 407 independence).
2.  **Segregation Violations:** The AR clerk has physical custody of assets (customer checks), performs recordkeeping (updating subledger), and executes reconciliation (preparing bank statement).
3.  **Corrective Actions:** Appoint an independent financial expert to lead the Audit Committee, and divide the clerk's tasks among separate employees.
