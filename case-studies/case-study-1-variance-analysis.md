# Case Study 1: Variance Analysis

## Scenario
A company uses a standard costing system. Standard requirements for one unit are:
*   Standard Quantity: 4 lbs of Material X at $5/lb.
*   Actual Results for 1,000 units produced:
    *   Material X purchased and used: 4,200 lbs at $5.20/lb.

## Required Tasks
1.  Calculate the Direct Material Price Variance.
2.  Calculate the Direct Material Usage Variance.
3.  Analyze the results and suggest possible operational reasons.

3.  **Operational Analysis:** The purchasing department bought higher-priced materials, but this did not reduce waste (since usage was also unfavorable). It indicates inefficiencies or machine errors.
