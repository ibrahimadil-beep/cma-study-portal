# Section D Practice Exam Drills

### Question 1
The production supervisor's salary of a manufacturing plant is classified as:
*   (A) Direct product cost for a single unit of product.
*   (B) Indirect product cost for a single unit of product, but direct cost for the production department.
*   (C) Period cost to be expensed immediately on the Income Statement.
*   (D) Unexpired asset capitalized in inventory indefinitely.

**Correct Answer: (B)**
*Explanation: According to the Object-Dependent Rule, a supervisor's salary is directly traceable to the production department as a whole, but is an indirect product cost when allocating to a specific unit of product.*
