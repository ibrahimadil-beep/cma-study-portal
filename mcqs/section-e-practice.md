# Section E Practice Exam Drills

### Question 1
To prevent accounts receivable lapping frauds, which segregation of duties is most critical?
*   (A) Authorizing sales transactions and recording sales entries.
*   (B) Receiving cash collections and posting entries to individual customer accounts.
*   (C) Reconciling bank statements and authorizing bad-debt write-offs.
*   (D) Custody of raw materials and recording work-in-process inventory.

**Correct Answer: (B)**
*Explanation: Lapping occurs when an employee steals a cash payment from Customer A and covers it up by applying Customer B's payment. Separating asset custody (receiving collections) from recordkeeping (posting customer accounts) prevents this fraud.*
