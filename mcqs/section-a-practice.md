# Section A Practice Exam Drills

### Question 1
Under accrual accounting, when is revenue generally recognized?
*   (A) When cash is collected from the customer.
*   (B) When the contract is signed by both parties.
*   (C) When a performance obligation is satisfied by transferring goods or services.
*   (D) At the end of the fiscal year when adjusting entries are made.

**Correct Answer: (C)**
*Explanation: Accrual accounting recognizes revenue in the period in which the performance obligation is satisfied, regardless of when cash is collected.*
