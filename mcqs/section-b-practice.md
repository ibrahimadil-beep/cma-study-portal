# Section B Practice Exam Drills

### Question 1
Which of the following is the standard starting point for compiling a company's Master Budget?
*   (A) Production Budget
*   (B) Sales Budget
*   (C) Cash Budget
*   (D) Direct Materials Purchase Budget

**Correct Answer: (B)**
*Explanation: Sales projections drive all operating activities, including production and resource purchasing. Hence, the Sales Budget is the foundational step.*
