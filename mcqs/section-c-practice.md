# Section C Practice Exam Drills

### Question 1
A standard costing system shows a highly unfavorable direct material usage variance. Which of the following is the most likely cause?
*   (A) Purchasing department bought lower-grade material at a discount.
*   (B) Machine operator efficiency increased.
*   (C) Standard price was set too low by the engineering department.
*   (D) Production volume was lower than budgeted.

**Correct Answer: (A)**
*Explanation: Buying lower-grade material at a discount saves money initially (favorable price variance) but often results in high scrap rates and material waste (unfavorable usage variance).*
